Mailbox (reply tracking)

The app reads your inbox over IMAP every few minutes, looks for mail from companies you've marked Applied (and anything that reads like recruiter traffic), and has Claude classify it. Nothing is sent from your account unless you turn on the email notification. Gmail: turn on 2-step verification, then create an app password and paste it here, not your normal password.
